Three things it does that matter.

It doesn't guess

It says “I don't know” instead of inventing an answer.

The agent can only answer from facts you gave it. Anything else it doesn't answer — it asks the customer whether to pass the question to you, then sends it to your Telegram. You reply as yourself, and your answer is saved so it knows next time.

Bolalar nevrologi bormi?
Bu haqda ma'lumotim yo'q. Xohlasangiz, klinikadan so'rab, javobini yozaman — so'raymi?
You can check it

Every answer shows where it came from.

Open any answer and you see which of your own facts produced it, and whether you typed that fact or it was read from a file you uploaded. Nothing the agent says comes from anywhere but your own information.

Both scripts, one conversation

Uzbek, Russian and English — including mixed messages.

Customers here switch language mid-sentence and write Uzbek in Cyrillic as often as Latin. The agent follows the customer rather than making them choose, and answers in whatever they wrote in.

How it works

1 Add what you know A photo of your price list, a document, or just type the facts.
2 Check what it read See every fact beside the text it came from. Fix or drop anything wrong.
3 Test it yourself Ask it the questions your customers ask, before anyone can reach it.
4 Connect Telegram One token from BotFather, and it starts answering.

About fifteen minutes. Nothing goes live until you've tested it yourself.
