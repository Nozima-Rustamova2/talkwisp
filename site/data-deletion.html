Data deletion

Last updated 16 September 2026

You can ask us to delete what we hold about you, whether you are a business using Talkwisp or someone who messaged a business's bot. This page says how to ask, what actually happens, and what we cannot reach.

How to ask

Email info@talkwisp.uz with the subject Data deletion, and tell us which you are:

  • A business. Write from the email address you sign in with. That is how we know it is you.
  • A customer who messaged a bot. Tell us which business's bot you messaged and your Telegram username, or send /start to that bot and mention your request — we can then match your account.

We may ask one follow-up question if we cannot tell which records are yours. We will not ask for identity documents.

What happens then

A person does it by hand. There is no delete button and no automated job. Someone runs database statements to remove your rows and edits our three log files to remove your lines.

We say this because it is true and because it is what makes thirty days a commitment we can meet rather than a number copied from another company's policy.

We will complete it within 30 days of your request, and email you when it is done.

What gets deleted

If you are a customer

  • Every message you sent the bot and every reply, from our message log.
  • Your Telegram ID, first name and username wherever they appear.
  • Questions of yours the agent could not answer, from our gap log.
  • Any question of yours passed to the business, including the conversation context stored with it.
  • Any orders you placed, and our reference to any payment screenshot you sent.

If you are a business

  • Your account, sign-in email and any active sessions.
  • Your business name, agent settings and greetings.
  • Your Telegram bot token, and the owner records for everyone who claimed the bot.
  • All of your knowledge: typed facts, uploaded files and the text extracted from them, and your payment details including your card number.
  • Your customers' conversations, orders and escalations.

Deleting your business account deletes your customers' data with it. If you want to keep your knowledge, export or copy it before asking.

What we cannot reach

Being precise about this is more useful than promising more than we can do.

  • Telegram's own copy of the conversation. The chat exists in Telegram between you and the bot, on Telegram's servers, under Telegram's policy. Deleting our records does not remove it from your Telegram app or theirs. You can delete the chat yourself in Telegram.
  • Payment screenshots. We never stored the image — only a Telegram reference to it. We delete the reference; the picture stays on Telegram until it is deleted there.
  • Google's processing of past requests. Customer questions were sent to Google's Gemini API to be answered. What Google retains is governed by Google's terms, not ours, and we cannot delete it on your behalf.
  • Infrastructure snapshots at our hosting provider. We do not run backups ourselves, so there is no second copy we hold. Our provider may keep low-level snapshots we neither control nor can search.

Deleting some of it

You do not have to delete everything. A business can remove individual facts, sources and payment details from the Knowledge screen at any time, and remove an owner's access in Settings. If you want part of your data removed rather than all of it, say which part when you write.

If you just want the messages to stop

Block the bot in Telegram, or simply stop writing to it. The agent never messages anyone first — it only replies to a message you send. Blocking does not delete what is already stored; ask us if you want that too.
