Privacy policy

Last updated 16 September 2026

Talkwisp provides an AI assistant that businesses connect to their own Telegram bot. This page describes what we store, who else it passes through, and how to have it removed. It is written from what the software actually does, not from a template.

Talkwisp is operated from Tashkent, Uzbekistan. Contact: info@talkwisp.uz.

Two different people are covered here

Businesses sign up for Talkwisp, add information about themselves, and connect a Telegram bot.

Customers are the people who message that business's bot. They do not have a Talkwisp account and never interact with us directly. The business decides what their agent knows; we run the software behind it.

What we store about customers

When someone messages a business's bot, we record the following. Every item is something the software genuinely writes down — this is a list of fields, not a category.

WhatWhy it exists
Telegram chat and user ID To reply in the right conversation and to recognise a returning customer.
First name and Telegram username, where the account has them So the business can see who asked, and reach them again. Recorded from September 2026 onward.
The language code Telegram reports for the account A weak hint only; the reply language is decided from the text.
Every message sent to the bot, and every reply So the business can read the conversation, and so we can tell whether the agent answered well.
Questions the agent could not answer, with the internal match scores To show the business what its agent is missing.
Questions passed to a human, with the few messages before them An owner cannot answer a question without the context it came in.
Orders: what was ordered, the amount, the status and timestamps Someone who paid must not be lost.
A Telegram reference to a payment screenshot — see below Evidence for the business that a payment was sent.

We do not store payment screenshots. When a customer sends one, Telegram keeps the image and gives us a short reference to it — a file_id. That is the only thing in our database. We never download the image, never copy it to our servers, and never read it with software. When the business views it, Telegram serves it to them directly.

We also never see or handle any money. Payments happen card to card between the customer and the business.

What we store about businesses

  • The email address used to sign in, and sign-in sessions.
  • The business name, and any agent name, greeting or tone settings.
  • The Telegram bot token, so the software can operate the bot.
  • The Telegram ID and first name of each person who claims the bot as an owner — up to three.
  • Everything the business adds as knowledge: typed facts, uploaded files and the text extracted from them, and payment details such as a card number, card holder name and bank.

Who else processes this

We use a small number of other companies. Each one is listed with what it actually receives.

Google — Gemini API

This is the most significant one, and it works three different ways depending on what the data is:

  • Customer questions are sent to Google. Every question a customer asks is sent to the Gemini API twice: once to convert it into a numeric representation for searching, and once to write the reply, together with the business information needed to answer it.
  • Files a business uploads are sent to Google. Price lists and documents, including photographs of them, are read by Gemini's vision model so their contents can be turned into facts.
  • Payment screenshots are never sent to Google. They are not read by any model, by design. They exist only for the business to look at.

Google processes this under its own terms for the Gemini API.

Telegram

The conversation happens on Telegram, so Telegram necessarily handles every message and holds its own copy under its own privacy policy. Payment screenshots are stored on Telegram's servers rather than ours.

Google Cloud, Cloudflare, Resend

  • Google Cloud hosts the server and the database.
  • Cloudflare sits in front of our website and sees requests to it.
  • Resend delivers sign-in emails to business owners. It receives the owner's email address. It is not involved in customer conversations.

How long we keep it

Nothing is deleted automatically. There is no retention period, no scheduled clean-up and no cascade. Data stays until someone asks us to remove it, and then a person removes it by hand.

We would rather say that plainly than state a number our software does not enforce. If this page claimed "we delete after 12 months", nothing in the system would make that true.

We do not run database backups, so there is no second copy of your data for us to find later. Our hosting provider may keep infrastructure-level snapshots that we do not control or have access to.

Deleting your data

Anyone can ask, whether they are a business or a customer who messaged one. The data deletion page explains exactly how to ask, what happens, and what we cannot reach.

Legal basis and your rights

We process this information in order to provide the service a business asked for, and because a customer chose to start a conversation with that business's bot. You can ask us what we hold about you, ask for it to be corrected, or ask for it to be deleted, by writing to info@talkwisp.uz.

If you messaged a business's bot, that business is also responsible for how it uses what you told them. We provide the software; they decide what to do with the conversation.

Children

Talkwisp is a tool for businesses and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes to this page

If we change what we store or who processes it, we will change this page and update the date at the top.
