What the service is

Talkwisp runs an assistant on a Telegram bot that you own. You give it information about your business; it answers customer questions using only that information.

It answers only from what you gave it. When a question is not covered by your information, the agent says it does not know rather than guessing, and offers to pass the question to you. That is the central behaviour of the product and we build to keep it true.

It is still software using a language model. It can misunderstand a question, pick the wrong fact, or word an answer badly. You are responsible for checking what it says about your business, and the product gives you the tools to do that — a test console, a record of every conversation, and the source behind every fact.

Payments

We never handle money. Talkwisp does not process payments, hold funds, or act as a payment provider or agent. When a customer chooses to pay, the agent shows them the card details you provided and a unique amount, and they transfer it to you directly.
